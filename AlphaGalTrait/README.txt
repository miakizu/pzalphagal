Alpha Gal Trait

For Project Zomboid Build 42.20 and newer.

Install the AlphaGalTrait folder in your Zomboid mods folder, then enable "Alpha Gal Trait" in the Mods menu.

The Alpha Gal trait costs -10 points. Red meat adds exposure based on how much is eaten. A quarter serving stays in the mild range, medium amounts can cause a moderate reaction, and a full normal serving reaches the severe range. Reactions begin after a two in-game hour delay by default. Severe symptoms then ramp progressively for about three in-game hours, raising body temperature, draining health toward 50%, and worsening exhaustion and other moodles without the old instant stat drop. At the fatal end stage, "I can't breathe...." is rendered above the character for about five real seconds before death.

Chicken, turkey, other poultry, fish, seafood, and other non-mammalian meats are excluded.

Reaction chances, severe reaction length, and exposure decay can be changed in Sandbox Options under Alpha Gal Trait.

Mod compatibility details are in README_API.txt.
